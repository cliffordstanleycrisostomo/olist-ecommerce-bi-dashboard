# Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard
End-to-end Business Intelligence project using PostgreSQL and Power BI on the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset.

## Project Overview
This is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project using  PostgreSQL and Power BI to analyze the Brazilian Olist E-commerce marketplace dataset. The project translates raw marketplace data into an interactive dashboard, analyzing the overall sales performance, product performance, and customer behavior through four interactive Power BI dashboard pages to support business decision-making.

## Business Objectives
• Analyze market performance
• Identify top-performing product categories
• Assess customer purchasing behavior
• Monitor monthly sales trends
• Support business decision-making through interactive dashboards

## Dataset
Dataset Source: [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)
The dataset contains Olist Brazilian marketplace covering transactions from September 2016 through September 2018, including customers, orders, products, payments, reviews, and sellers.

## Dashboard Pages
### Executive Overview 
A high-level overview of the marketplace performance, covering revenue, customers, orders, sales trends, and regional distributions.


### Product Performance
Evaluates product categories by revenue, number of units sold, and customer ratings to identify top-performing and underperforming categories.


### Customer Analysis
Analyzes customer distribution, geographic revenue contribution, and customer purchasing behavior across Brazilian states and cities.


### Sales Performance Analysis 
Examines revenue trends, order volume, seasonal performance, and order fulfillment status over time.

## Key Insights
• Revenue peaked during late 2017 and early 2018.
• São Paulo generated the highest revenue and customer concentration.
• Health & Beauty was the strongest-performing product category.
• Cancelled orders represented less than 1% of transactions.
• Customer review scores remained consistently above 4.0 across most categories.
